Read before using

Fixed-price rows price awards off the cash fare, so a point is worth about the same on every flight and the multiplier works well.

Variable-price rows use award charts, dynamic pricing or partner awards. Domestic economy is often worth less than the average, while premium cabins on long flights can be worth far more. Treat the multiplier as a floor there.
