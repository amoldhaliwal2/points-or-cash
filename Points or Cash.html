Read before using

Green rows price awards off the cash fare, so a point is worth about the same on every flight and the multiplier works well.

Amber rows use award charts, dynamic pricing or partner awards. Domestic economy is often worth less than the average, while premium cabins on long flights can be worth far more. Treat the multiplier as a floor there.

A cash ticket also earns points back, and a Southwest award still charges $5.60 in fees one way. Spirit isn't in TPG's table, so it's left out.

Values are The Points Guy's October 2026 estimates (multiplier = 100 ÷ cents per point, rounded). They are third-party estimates, not airline-published values, and TPG updates them monthly. Source: TPG monthly valuations.
